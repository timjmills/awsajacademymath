Grade 1 Weekly Pacing Guide · 2026–27

THE TRACKS ARE FLEXIBLE · MOVE BETWEEN THEM IN ANY WEEK. A child or a whole class can change track whenever the work stops fitting — move up towards Standard when they are secure, down to Priority or Intervention when they are struggling. You do not have to wait for a unit to end, and a child can move more than once in a year. Every track teaches every essential step and all three finish each unit together, so a change at a unit boundary is always clean — nothing repeated, nothing missed. Mid-unit, compare the two columns for that week: a lighter track may sit a step or two behind, so if you move a child up, pick those steps up in a support block; moving down never loses a lesson.

Three strands, one map. Yellow highlight = power standard. Orange BUILD = CCSS custom lesson still to be written. ADAPT = UK content to adapt for US before teaching. Pink = support options / support block (pick one).
